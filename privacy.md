# Privacy Policy - AI Solver Poker Tracker

Effective Date: August 17, 2025

AI Solver Poker Tracker values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

## Information We Do Not Collect
- We do not collect, store, or share any personal information such as name, email, or phone number.
- All poker hand data, session logs, and statistics remain on your device.

## Third-Party Services
- Our app uses **RevenueCat** for subscription management. RevenueCat relies on Apple's App Store infrastructure to process purchases. We do not see or store your payment information.
- Apple may collect limited data related to your in-app purchases as described in Apple's privacy policy.

## Data Storage
- All data generated in the app (e.g., hand tracking, session results) is stored locally on your device.
- You may delete the app at any time to remove all locally stored data.

## Children’s Privacy
- Our app is not directed at children under the age of 13.
- We do not knowingly collect data from children.
